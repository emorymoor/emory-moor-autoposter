Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Emory Moor AutoPoster

Emory Moor AutoPoster is an application designed to help create, schedule, and publish promotional content related to books on Pinterest.

Information We Access

The application may access information provided through the Pinterest API, including:

  • Pinterest account information necessary for authentication;
  • Pinterest boards;
  • Pins and content associated with the connected account;
  • permissions granted by the user through Pinterest OAuth.

The application does not request or store Pinterest passwords.

How Information Is Used

Information accessed through Pinterest is used only to provide the application's functionality, including:

  • selecting Pinterest boards;
  • creating and publishing Pins;
  • scheduling book-related content;
  • managing content created through the application.

Pinterest data is not sold or shared with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Authentication

The application uses Pinterest's official OAuth authentication process. Access tokens are used only to communicate with the Pinterest API and provide requested application functionality.

Data Storage

The application stores only the information necessary to provide its functionality. Information received through the Pinterest API is not retained longer than necessary.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell, rent, or provide Pinterest user data to unrelated third parties.

Data Deletion

Users may revoke the application's access to their Pinterest account through their Pinterest account settings.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data-processing practices change.
